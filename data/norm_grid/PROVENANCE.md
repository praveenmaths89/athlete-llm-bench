# Centile grid

`cmj_jump_height_centile_grid.csv`: reference centiles (P3, P10, P25, P50, P75, P90, P97) of impulse-momentum countermovement-jump height (cm), by sex and age in 0.5-year steps, from a GAMLSS centile model fitted in earlier work by the authors on the same athlete population (reference to be added after review). The grid is used as a lookup; no model is fitted in this repository. Columns are those of the published supplementary table: test_type, gender, variable, age, P3..P97, n_tests, n_athletes, sample_label, expected_se_z_p3.
The centiles are in-sample with respect to the athletes in the benchmark (stated in the paper's Limitations).
