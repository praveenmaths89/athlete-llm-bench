# Data statement

**Source.** Countermovement-jump tests recorded with force plates in a football academy setting, supplied under a data-sharing agreement. The release covers 408 tests of 264 athletes (men and women, ages 8.8 to 37.9 years at test) and 1,050 individual trials, between November 2022 and July 2026.

**Collection and consent.** Tests were collected as part of routine athlete monitoring. Informed consent for the use of these data was obtained from all participants, and from a parent or guardian in the case of minors.

**De-identification.** Names, the club, the original profile identifier and the date of birth are removed. Each athlete has a pseudonymous identifier (salted hash; the salt is not released). Test dates, age at test, height, body mass, age band, sex and every trial value are kept as recorded. Remaining risk: exact dates together with age and sex of minors are quasi-identifiers. Users must not attempt re-identification and must not link the tables with other data about these athletes.

**Intended use.** Evaluating question answering and tool use over longitudinal measurements. Not intended for decisions about individual athletes, and not for training models that identify individuals.

**Licence.** The released tables, questions and cached responses are distributed under the Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International licence (CC BY-NC 4.0). The code is released under the Apache License 2.0.

**Known limits.** One sport, one test, one device family; female athletes are fewer; typical error is estimated from 2 to 3 trials per test; centiles come from a model fitted on the same population.
